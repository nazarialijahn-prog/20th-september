SPORTS

football

Each team usually has 11 players on the field, including one goalkeeper. A football match normally lasts 90 minutes, divided into two halves of 45 minutes. Players usually cannot use their hands or arms to control the ball. The goalkeeper is the main exception inside their penalty area.

volleyball

Volleyball is a popular team sport played by two teams on a court divided by a net. Each team usually has six players on the court at one time. The main goal is to send the ball over the net and make it touch the opponent's court.The serve starts each rally.

golf

Golf is a popular sport played by hitting a small ball with a club into a series of holes. A standard golf course usually has 18 holes. Golf can be played by people of different ages and skill levels. The main goal is to complete the course using as few strokes as possible.

tabletennis

Table tennis is a popular indoor sport played with small paddles and a lightweight ball. It is also commonly called ping-pong. Table tennis can be played by two players (singles) or four players (doubles). The game is played on a rectangular table divided by a net. A serve starts each point.

basketball

Basketball is a popular team sport played between two teams. Each team tries to score points by putting the ball through the opponent's basket. Basketball was invented in 1891 by James Naismith in the United States. A basketball team has five players on the court at one time.

tennis

Tennis is a popular sport played with a racket and a ball. It can be played between two players (singles) or four players (doubles). Players use a tennis racket to hit the ball over the net. The main goal is to hit the ball into the opponent's court so that they cannot return it correctly.
